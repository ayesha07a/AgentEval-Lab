# Helio Transit Authority: Rider Policy

Bicycles are permitted on all lines except during peak hours, defined as 7:00 to 9:30 on weekday mornings and 16:30 to 19:00 on weekday evenings. Pets must travel in a carrier. Service animals are exempt from the carrier rule. Lost items are held at the Kestrel Heights station office for 30 days before being donated.
