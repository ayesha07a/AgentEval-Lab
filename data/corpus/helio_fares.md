# Helio Transit Authority: Fares

The Helio Transit Authority (HTA) operates the Harbor Line, the Ridge Line, and the Canal Line. A single adult fare costs 2.40 credits. Children under 6 ride free. The monthly pass costs 68 credits and allows unlimited rides on all three lines. Fares are paid with a Helio Card, which has a one-time issue fee of 5 credits.
