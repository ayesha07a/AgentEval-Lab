# Helio Transit Authority: Operations

The Harbor Line opened in 2009 and runs 14 stations from Port Alder to Kestrel Heights. The Ridge Line opened in 2015 and has 9 stations. The Canal Line opened in 2021 and is the only line that runs 24 hours a day. Trains on the Harbor Line arrive every 6 minutes during peak hours and every 12 minutes off-peak. The main depot is located in Marrow Yard.
